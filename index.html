About Me

My work has always started with people. I began designing training as an AmeriCorps Curriculum Specialist in West Virginia and a Peace Corps Volunteer in the Commonwealth of Dominica. As a government employee, I spent eleven years at the Federal Highway Administration running onboarding, mentoring, and team workshops for a nationwide workforce.

Today I design technical training as an Instructional Designer at Dominion Energy. I also have my own AI-Consulting business called Sicay Consulting where I help leaders handle the human side of AI adoption. I hold an M.Ed. in Learning Design & Technology and a B.A. in Conflict Analysis & Intervention, both from James Madison University.
